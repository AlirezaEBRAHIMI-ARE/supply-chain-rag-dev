Shipping Policy

We ship orders promptly after purchase confirmation. 
Domestic orders (United Kingdom) typically arrive within 3-5 business days. 
International orders to European countries arrive within 5-10 business days. 
Orders shipped outside Europe may take 10-20 business days depending on customs processing.

Final delivery to the recipient's door can be significantly affected by weekends 
and public holidays, as courier services do not operate on these days. Weekends 
and public holidays are therefore excluded from delivery time estimates and should 
not be counted toward expected delivery windows.

During high-demand periods (September through December), delivery times may 
extend by an additional 2-5 business days due to increased order volume.

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on destination and order weight.
