Customs and International Shipping FAQ

International customers are responsible for any customs duties, taxes, or 
import fees charged by their country upon arrival. These charges are not 
included in the order total and are collected by the carrier or local customs office.

Orders may be delayed at customs for inspection, particularly for shipments 
to countries with strict import regulations. Average customs clearance time 
is 2-5 business days but can extend further during peak seasons.

We are not responsible for delays caused by customs authorities.
