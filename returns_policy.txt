Returns and Refunds Policy

Customers may return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, 
provided items are unused and in original packaging.

Damaged or defective items must be reported within 7 days of delivery, 
with photo evidence, to qualify for a free replacement or full refund.

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item.
Sale items and personalized products are final sale and not eligible for return.
